License Finder

Evaluate options for licensing your software, writing, art, music, and other creative work

The License Finder is designed to help you find an open source or Creative Commons license that fits your wishes. Start by telling us whether you are licensing software. You will then see only the licenses and questions that apply. It gives general information, not legal advice.
