Creative Commons License Finder

Evaluate options for licensing your writing, art, music, and other creative work

The License Finder is designed to help you find a Creative Commons license that fits your wishes. It covers the six main CC licenses plus CC0, and it gives general information, not legal advice. Creative Commons licenses are not recommended for software.
